**BLUEFOR EYES ONLY.** *Fictional training scenario.*

> **FORMAT NOTE (delete before printing):** Format as a personal letter (Tongue and Quill, ch. 15; summary in `references/tongue-and-quill-summary.md` §4). Use [Office of the President] letterhead (set in `game.toml` `[[tq]]`). Date it in "Day Month Year" form; the in-game date is D−4. Indent paragraphs and don't number them. Open punctuation: no comma after the salutation or the close. `tools/tq/build_tq.py` reads this layout: the date line, the address lines, the salutation (`Dear`), the body, `Sincerely`, a line of `&nbsp;` before the signature block, and the attachment.

// BLUEFOR SECRET //

OFFICE OF THE PRESIDENT

1 January 2027

[Title of the first commander]\
[Title of the second commander]

Dear [General Last and Commander Last]

[Why the head of state is writing, and that the objectives are enclosed.]

[The situation as BLUEFOR sees it: what the enemy wants, and what the country can and can't afford.]

[Forces and support: what the commanders have, what outside support to expect, and the limits on it.]

[Limits and authorities: the standing orders in `scenario/00-overview.md` §7.2, and the authority to negotiate.]

[Intent: what success looks like, and what the head of state won't accept.]

Sincerely

&nbsp;

[FIRST M. LAST]\
President of [Blue nation]

&nbsp;

Attachment:\
[The Republic's objectives] (BLUEFOR Team Packet, §1)

// BLUEFOR SECRET //
