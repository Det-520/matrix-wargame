**REDFOR EYES ONLY.** *Fictional training scenario.*

> **FORMAT NOTE (delete before printing):** Format as an official memorandum (Tongue and Quill, ch. 14; summary in `references/tongue-and-quill-summary.md` §3). Use [Red headquarters] letterhead (set in `game.toml` `[[tq]]`). Date it in "Day Month Year" form; the in-game date is D−4. Number the paragraphs as shown. `tools/tq/build_tq.py` reads this layout: the date line, MEMORANDUM FOR, FROM, SUBJECT, numbered paragraphs (`1\.` and `a.`), a line of `&nbsp;` before the signature block, and the attachment.

// REDFOR SECRET //

[RED NATION] MINISTRY OF DEFENSE

1 January 2027

MEMORANDUM FOR COMMANDER, [RED FORWARD FORCES]

FROM: [Chief of Staff], [Red armed forces]

SUBJECT: Strategic Guidance for Operations in [the theater]

1\. [The tasking, in two or three sentences: what the government has directed, and that the objectives are at the attachment.]

2\. Situation. [What the other side has done, and why it threatens REDFOR's interests. Keep to what REDFOR knows.]

3\. Forces and sustainment. [What is in theater, how long it can sustain high tempo, and why requests for forces should go in early.]

4\. Authorities. Without further approval you are not authorized to:

a. [A limit from `scenario/00-overview.md` §7.1, e.g. put ground forces on enemy territory.]

b. [Another limit.]

If the situation requires any of these, send the request to me with your rationale.

These limits apply to military operations only. You are authorized to negotiate in the Government's name, directly or through intermediaries, and you do not need further approval to open talks or conclude an agreement.

5\. Political considerations. [Domestic support, international standing, and what will erode them.]

6\. Partners. [What each ally or host nation has and hasn't granted.]

7\. Enemy. [What REDFOR knows about the enemy's forces and weaknesses, pointing to the intelligence estimate.]

8\. Intent. [One or two sentences: the end state and the acceptable cost.]

&nbsp;

[FIRST M. LAST], General, [Red armed forces]\
[Chief of Staff]

&nbsp;

Attachment:\
[Government's objectives] (REDFOR Team Packet, §1)

// REDFOR SECRET //
