## 2. Inject pool [PROPOSED; at most one per turn, the lead adjudicator's choice]

*Ten to fifteen injects. Most of the generic ones below work in any scenario once the names are filled in; add scenario-specific ones that reward or punish particular plans. Keep a bounding directive in every pool.*

| ID | Inject | Purpose | Suggested timing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I-01 | **Storm front**: heavy weather over [the theater] for one turn. −1 to air COAs below the weather and to small-boat COAs. | Friction, forces adaptation | Turn 2–3 |
| I-02 | **Neutral ship hit** by a mine or missile in [the chokepoint]. Both sides blame each other. International Opinion moves against whoever the evidence points to. | Consequence of fighting near shipping | After any mining or anti-ship COA |
| I-03 | **Leaked strike video** shows civilian casualties (the white cell picks the side that caused the most civilian risk). International Opinion ±1. | Restraint and legitimacy | Turn 3–4 |
| I-04 | **[Red] legislature hearing** questions the war's legality. Domestic Will −1 unless REDFOR's next COA addresses it. | Political front for REDFOR | Turn 3–5 |
| I-05 | **An exiled opposition leader** calls for an uprising in [Blue nation]. Regime Stability −1 unless BLUEFOR's next COA counters it. | Pressure on BLUEFOR's home front | Turn 2–4 |
| I-06 | **An officer defects**, carrying the location of one hidden counter of the side that suffered the defection. | Rewards exploitation of internal division | After a successful COA aimed at loyalties |
| I-07 | **[Backer]'s convoy** heads for [Blue nation] under escort. REDFOR must decide whether to stop it (it needs national authority). | Great-power dilemma: objectives vs. escalation | Turn 3–5 |
| I-08 | **Pirates or criminals** hijack a cargo ship while both sides are busy. | Second-order effect of chaos | Any turn shipping is disrupted |
| I-09 | **Cyberattack** knocks a host nation's civilian power offline for a day. Its government demands protection. | Host-nation pressure | Turn 3–4 |
| I-10 | **GPS jamming** across the theater. −1 to GPS-dependent precision COAs for one turn unless a team argues around it. | Space and EMS awareness | Turn 4–6 |
| I-11 | **A wavering third party offers access**, with conditions. Once granted, the access stays even if the tracks later move (`RULES.md` §5). | Opportunity plus a dilemma | When International Opinion favors the side asking |
| I-12 | **Mediation offer**: a neutral state offers to host talks. | Off-ramp; tests whether teams can win without fighting | Phase III |
| I-13 | **Bounding directive** (use if needed): national command forbids an escalating behavior (e.g. "no further strikes on [a target set]"). | **[CANON tool]** to keep the game on the rails | As needed |

**Track triggers** (`scenario/00-overview.md` §5.1) produce their own injects automatically when reached.
