## 3. Objective status card

The tracks and the map don't record everything the objectives check. The logger keeps an **objective status card** (`handouts/whitecell/forms.md` §3) for the rest and updates it in the resolve step with the tracks, at no extra time cost.

**The card is white cell only.** It lists items that only the hidden objectives care about, so a card placed beside the public tracks would tell each team what the other is trying to do. Keep it with the logger, face down.

| Item | Records | Objectives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [e.g. talks on a political settlement] | None / formal talks begun / agreement reached | [R2, B1] |
| [e.g. outside shipments interdicted] | Count, with turn | [R3] |

A player agreement (`RULES.md` §6) changes the card only once both teams have confirmed it to the white cell. If they disagree about what they agreed, the logger's note from the parley (`RULES.md` §6.1) settles it.
